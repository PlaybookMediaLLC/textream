Privacy Policy · Updated November 26, 2026

Privacy, without fine print.

Dante for Creators is published by Playbook Interactive Media LLC. The app requires no account, contains no ads or analytics SDKs, and does not send your scripts, recordings, or settings to Playbook Interactive Media LLC servers.

Your scripts stay on your device

Your Creator Script Library, presets, and preferences remain in local storage on your Mac, iPhone, or iPad.

No ads, accounts, or telemetry

Dante for Creators includes zero advertising, behavioral tracking, analytics SDKs, or developer-hosted user accounts.

100% offline option on Mac

Choose Apple Speech or download optional on-device AI speech models on macOS so your voice audio never leaves your Mac.

Data collected by Playbook Interactive Media LLC

Playbook Interactive Media LLC does not collect, sell, or share personal data through Dante for Creators. The app operates without a developer-operated account system, analytics service, advertising network, or cloud script backend.

When you opt into system services or third-party downloads—such as Apple Speech Recognition or optional Hugging Face model downloads—those providers process standard network connection data under their own privacy policies.

Microphone and Apple Speech Recognition

Smart Follow (Word Tracking) and Voice-Activated scrolling request microphone access when enabled. On iPhone and iPad, Record mode also captures microphone audio for your saved camera video.

When you choose the Apple Speech engine, Dante uses Apple’s Speech framework to match spoken words to your script. Depending on your device, language, and OS version, Apple may process audio on-device or on Apple servers under the Apple Privacy Policy. Playbook Interactive Media LLC never receives your audio or transcripts.

Classic Read mode runs without microphone or speech-recognition permissions. You can change permissions at any time in macOS System Settings or iOS Settings.

Local Speech Models & Hugging Face Downloads (macOS)

On macOS, you can optionally switch the speech engine to Local Model to run speech recognition entirely on your Mac so microphone audio never leaves your device.

  • Dante for Creators does not bundle model weights inside the app download.
  • When you choose to download a catalog model or resolve a public user-specified Hugging Face repository, Dante connects over HTTPS to huggingface.co to query repository metadata and download non-executable .gguf / .bin model weight files.
  • Every download is verified against its pinned SHA-256 checksum before activation, stored locally in Application Support, and automatically excluded from Time Machine and iCloud backups.
  • Hugging Face receives standard HTTPS connection metadata (such as your IP address and requested model file) subject to the Hugging Face Privacy Policy. No scripts, recordings, or personal identifiers are ever sent to Hugging Face.

You can delete downloaded models at any time in Settings → Guidance → Manage Models.

iPhone and iPad camera recordings & Photos

Dante requests camera access only when you enable the camera preview or start Record mode. Read mode can run with the camera turned off.

The translucent teleprompter overlay is presenter-only: it appears on your screen while recording and is never burned into the exported video.

When you stop recording, Dante uses Photos add-only access to save your video without reading your existing library. If you open the saved-recordings gallery thumbnail, Dante separately requests Photos read/write access and filters the view strictly to asset identifiers created by Dante so you can preview, trim, or delete your takes.

While saving, a temporary copy is kept in local app storage excluded from device backups and automatically deleted once saved to Photos. If saving fails, Dante offers Retry Save or Discard Recording.

Scripts, library, and files

Your Creator Script Library and settings are stored locally on your device. On macOS, when you save a .dante script or import speaker notes from a PowerPoint .pptx file, all parsing happens strictly in-process on your Mac without uploading documents anywhere.

Remote Connection and Director Mode (macOS)

Remote Connection and Director Mode are disabled by default. When you turn either mode on in Settings, Dante starts a local-network HTTP and WebSocket server on your Mac so a tablet, phone, or producer’s browser on the same Wi-Fi network can view or control your teleprompter.

  • Connected devices on your LAN receive the active script text, reading progress, and display settings.
  • Director Mode allows an authorized browser session on your LAN to send or live-edit script text.
  • Local-network traffic is not end-to-end encrypted across the Wi-Fi segment.

Enable Remote Connection or Director Mode only on trusted networks with trusted devices, and turn them off when finished.

Screen sharing

Enabling Hide from Screen Sharing instructs macOS to exclude Dante’s teleprompter overlay from standard screen capture APIs. Because third-party capture drivers and external HDMI capture hardware vary, always verify your recording or broadcast setup before going live.

Retention, deletion & contact

Because Playbook Interactive Media LLC does not store your scripts, audio, or videos on external servers, you retain full local control. You can delete scripts from the Script Library, remove downloaded speech models in Settings, and delete recordings in Photos or the in-app gallery.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. For privacy questions or support requests, visit Dante for Creators Support.
