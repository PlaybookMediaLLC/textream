Download benchmarked local GGUF models like Nemotron 3.5 Streaming, Qwen3-ASR 1.7B, and Canary 180M Flash for 100% offline tracking with zero cloud audio.

Creator Library & Presets

Notch, Floating & Mirror Rigs

Pin below your MacBook notch, float anywhere with translucent glass, hide from screen sharing, or drive an external teleprompter mirror display.

iPhone & iPad Camera Capture

Record crisp video with a presenter-only teleprompter overlay beside the lens—your script never appears in the final video saved to Photos.

LAN Remote & Director Mode

Control or monitor scripts from any tablet or producer’s browser on the same Wi-Fi network with live delta updates and zero cloud accounts.
