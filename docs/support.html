Dante for Creators Support

Let’s get you back on script.

Quick setup tips and troubleshooting for Smart Follow, Local AI speech models, Creator Presets, overlays, iPhone and iPad camera recording, and local-network modes.

Quick fixes

01

Smart Follow (Word Tracking) will not start

Allow Dante for Creators under both Microphone and Speech Recognition in System Settings → Privacy & Security (macOS) or Settings (iOS). You can also switch to Classic mode for microphone-free scrolling.

02

Choosing a Local AI speech model on Mac

Open Settings → Guidance → Local Model → Manage Models. On Apple Silicon Macs, Nemotron 3.5 ASR Streaming 0.6B is ideal for live streaming, Qwen3-ASR 1.7B excels with strong accents, and Canary 180M Flash uses minimal memory. Intel Macs should use Apple Speech or lightweight models.

03

The overlay appears on the wrong monitor

Open Settings → Teleprompter. Choose Follow Mouse to move automatically between displays, or Fixed Display to lock the notch or fullscreen overlay to one screen.

04

Remote Connection or Director Mode will not connect

Ensure both devices are on the same trusted Wi-Fi network, enable the mode in Settings, and scan the QR code. If macOS Firewall prompts for incoming connections, allow Dante for Creators.

05

Importing PowerPoint or Keynote speaker notes

Drop any .pptx file with presenter notes directly onto the Dante editor window or open it via File → Open. For Keynote or Google Slides, export to PowerPoint (.pptx) first.

06

Hiding the overlay from screen sharing

Enable Hide from Screen Sharing in Settings → Teleprompter (also included in the Podcast and Keynote Creator Presets). Make a quick test recording to confirm compatibility with your capture tool.

07

iPhone or iPad recording and Photos access

Record mode needs Camera, Microphone, and Photos add-only permission. If a save fails, tap Retry Save in the Record session—Dante safely holds the pending take locally until saved or discarded.

08

Updates and Universal Purchase

Dante for Creators is a Universal Purchase across Mac, iPhone, and iPad. Install and update through the Apple App Store using the same Apple Account across your devices.

What to include in a support email

To help us resolve your issue quickly, include:

  • your macOS, iOS, or iPadOS version and Dante for Creators version;
  • your Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel), iPhone, or iPad model;
  • your selected Guidance mode (Apple Speech, Local Model name, Classic, or Voice-Activated);
  • steps to reproduce the issue (please remove private scripts before sending screenshots).
