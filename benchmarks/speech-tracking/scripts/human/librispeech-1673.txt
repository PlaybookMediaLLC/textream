A Laudable Regard For The Honor Of The First Proselyte Has Countenanced The Belief The Hope The Wish That The Ebionites Or At Least The Nazarenes Were Distinguished Only By Their Obstinate Perseverance In The Practice Of The Mosaic Rites. Their Churches Have Disappeared Their Books Are Obliterated Their Obscure Freedom Might Allow A Latitude Of Faith And The Softness Of Their Infant Creed Would Be Variously Moulded By The Zeal Or Prudence Of Three Hundred Years. Yet The Most Charitable Criticism Must Refuse These Sectaries Any Knowledge Of The Pure And Proper Divinity Of Christ. His Progress From Infancy To Youth And Manhood Was Marked By A Regular Increase In Stature And Wisdom And After A Painful Agony Of Mind And Body He Expired On The Cross. He Lived And Died For The Service Of Mankind But The Life And Death Of Socrates Had Likewise Been Devoted To The Cause Of Religion And Justice And Although The Stoic Or The Hero May Disdain The Humble Virtues Of Jesus The Tears Which He Shed Over His Friend And Country May Be Esteemed The Purest Evidence Of His Humanity. The Son Of A Virgin Generated By The Ineffable Operation Of The Holy Spirit Was A Creature Without Example Or Resemblance Superior In Every Attribute Of Mind And Body To The Children Of Adam.
